# HiGO-R2 vs GO-GPT: evaluation suite on GO-GPT's temporal test

Descriptive metrics of prediction files that were frozen before this suite existed (test look #3 in `runs/test_looks.md`). Nothing was tuned, selected or changed based on these numbers, and the suite records no new test look. Generated by `src/cafa6/eval_suite.py` (definitions in its module docstring); all numbers below come from the tsv files next to this report.

Ground truth: `data/proc_gogpt/gt_test.tsv`, 8,630 proteins (MF 2080, BP 5819, CC 3440 with annotations). Ontology `runs/gogpt_cafa5/ref/go-basic_gogpt.obo`, IA `runs/gogpt_cafa5/ref/IA.txt`, cafaeval protocol (prop=max, norm=cafa, tau 0.01..0.99). Brackets are paired protein bootstrap 95% CIs (1000 reps, seed 0). "mean" is the mean of the three aspects.

Rows: **HiGO-R2** (`runs/stacker_tv/l3_c0/pred_test_twin.npz`), **GO-GPT ten-sample** (`runs/gogpt_sample10/gogpt_sample10_freq.tsv.gz`), **GO-GPT beam** (`runs/gogpt_cafa5/preds_shard*.jsonl`). The two `[component]` rows are inputs of HiGO-R2 that exist as frozen test files (the train+val kNN prior and the HiGO head alone); they are not separate models and are not part of any test family.

## 0. Cross-check against the official cafaeval output

Fmax and IA-weighted Fmax of the three main rows were recomputed in memory and compared with `runs/test_look/look3_l3c0_twin/cafaeval/best_f.tsv` and `best_f_w.tsv`. **Check passed: max absolute difference 2.0e-04 over 18 values (required < 1e-3).** The GO-GPT rows match to 3e-15 on every checked value. The HiGO-R2 differences (up to 2.0e-04) come from the input file, not the scorer: the TSV given to cafaeval for HiGO-R2 had its scores rounded to 4 decimals, while this suite (like `cafa6.test_look`, which reported look #3) uses the submission format of `cafa6.evaluate.dense_to_long` (3 decimals, top 1,500 per protein; no protein has more than 1,448 scores >= 0.01, so only the rounding differs). Scoring a replica of that 4-decimal file reproduces cafaeval to 2e-15 for Fmax/Fmax_w and 2e-15 over all 18 checked HiGO-R2 values, including micro-F (`best_f_micro*.tsv`), the count-based S of `best_s.tsv` and s_w at its threshold. All tables below use the 3-decimal submission format for every row. Under that format the selected thresholds agree with cafaeval in 34 of 36 cases; the others are HiGO-R2 aspects where the 3- vs 4-decimal rounding moves the best threshold by one or a few steps. Handling of ties and zeros: scores are first rounded to 3 decimals and cut at 0.01 (the submission format cafaeval reads), so all scores lie on a 0.001 grid. In the micro AUPR every distinct score value is one operating point and all pairs tied at that value enter together; the last point (score 0) predicts every remaining pair of the aspect, so ground-truth pairs a model never scores still count against its recall. The protein-centric area uses the 99 thresholds of cafaeval and stops at the recall reached at tau = 0.01 (no extrapolation to recall 1).

**Flag: GO-GPT beam.** Beam predictions are sets with score 1, so its micro curve has two operating points (score 1 and score 0) and its protein curve one point; its AUPR values are close to precision x recall at that point. They are listed for completeness, but they measure one operating point rather than a ranking, and the beam rows of the AUPR tests in the p-value table carry the same flag. The ten-sample scores take only 11 values (0, 0.1, ..., 1), so its curves have at most 11 points.
